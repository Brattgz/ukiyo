The name

A floating world
in motion

The word ukiyo (浮世) originates from classical Japanese. Once meaning "the sorrowful world," it was reframed during the Edo period to describe the fleeting pleasures of everyday life — theatre, good food, refined beauty.


We carry that spirit into modern commerce. Small, modern pleasures that move quietly through everyday life — sourced with intention, delivered with care, arriving exactly where they are needed.

About Ukiyo Trading BV

A quiet trade,
carried out with precision

Ukiyo Trading BV is an Amsterdam-based trading house specialising in the import, export and pan-European distribution of premium consumer goods. We work at the intersection of established brand relationships and efficient logistics — moving product across borders with minimal friction and maximum reliability.

Our team combines deep knowledge of European import regulations, cold-chain handling, and retail-ready presentation — ensuring that what we deliver is not just product, but a service that reflects well on every party involved.

Our approach

Three guiding principles

01
🎯

Selectivity

We do not distribute everything. We work with a curated portfolio of trusted brands and partners, ensuring that each relationship is built on shared standards of quality and commercial integrity.

02
🚚

Reliability

Our in-house European logistics infrastructure ensures that orders move on time, with full tracking, proper documentation, and a single point of contact from order to delivery.

03

Discretion

Many of our relationships with manufacturers and retailers are long-term and built on trust. We operate quietly, without noise, as a professional extension of the brands we represent.
